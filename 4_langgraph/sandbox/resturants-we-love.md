# Restaurants We Love

## Bartholomew
Located at 1026 Mainland Street, Bartholomew is known for its great cocktails and sophisticated charcuterie plates. The ambiance is industrial-chic, making it a classy spot for intimate dates. They pride themselves on their adventurous yet well-executed cocktail list and diverse menu. Definitely a perfect go-to for wine and cocktails in Vancouver.

## Keefer Bar
Situated in Chinatown, the Keefer Bar has been recognized as one of Canada's best bars. It's known for its unique apothecary cocktails and lively atmosphere. While it can be a bit hit-or-miss with service, the creative drinks and vibrant setting make it a staple in Vancouver's cocktail scene. A great place for an evening to remember.

## Stock Room
A speakeasy-inspired bar located behind Hundy Burger in Yaletown, the Stock Room offers an exciting vibe along with fantastic cocktails. The atmosphere is great for intimate conversations, and the staff is known for being friendly and welcoming. The lighting and sound levels contribute to a perfect romantic setting.

## Arcana
This occult-themed cocktail bar located in Gastown offers an experience like no other. With a candlelit sanctuary ambiance, Arcana specializes in fusion cuisine and crafted cocktails, inspired by the Major Arcana of Tarot. The setting is unique, and the food is exceptional, making it a must-visit for a special date night.

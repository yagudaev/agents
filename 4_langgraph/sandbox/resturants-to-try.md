# Restaurants To Try

1. **Bartholomew** - A top choice due to its outstanding cocktails and charming ambiance. The focus on charcuterie and fine wines adds a unique touch to the experience.

2. **Stock Room** - Another excellent option known for its speakeasy vibe and impressive cocktail selection. The friendly staff and cozy setting make it a romantic spot.

3. **Arcana** - Its unique theme and exceptional food options make it stand out. Perfect for a more eclectic dining experience.

4. **Keefer Bar** - While highly rated, the service can be inconsistent; however, the creative cocktails make it worth a visit. Perfect for a lively evening out.

### Ratings:
- Bartholomew: 9.5/10
- Stock Room: 8.5/10
- Arcana: 8.0/10
- Keefer Bar: 7.5/10

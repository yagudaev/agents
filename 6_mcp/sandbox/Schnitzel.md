# Traditional German Schnitzel (Schweineschnitzel) Recipe

This recipe is for a traditional German Schnitzel made with pork, known as Schweineschnitzel, just like in German restaurants.

## Ingredients
- 4 boneless pork steaks or chops (for Austrian Wienerschnitzel use thin veal cutlets)
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper
- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour combined with 1 teaspoon salt
- 2 large eggs, lightly beaten
- 3/4 cup plain breadcrumbs
- Oil for frying (neutral-tasting with a high smoke point)

## Instructions
1. Place pork chops between two sheets of plastic wrap and pound until 1/4 inch thick using the flat side of a meat mallet.
2. Lightly season both sides with salt and freshly ground black pepper.
3. Prepare three shallow bowls: one with flour mixture, one with beaten eggs, and one with breadcrumbs.
4. Dip the pork chops first in flour, then egg, then breadcrumbs, coating all sides and edges. Do not press breadcrumbs into the meat; just coat gently and shake off excess.
5. Fry immediately in hot oil (about 330°F) using enough oil so the schnitzel can "swim" in it.
6. Fry for about 2-3 minutes on each side until deep golden brown.
7. Place briefly on a plate lined with paper towels to drain excess oil.
8. Serve garnished with slices of fresh lemon and parsley sprigs.

## Tips for Perfect Schnitzel
- Pound the meat thin to ensure quick frying and a crispy crust.
- Coat the meat gently with breadcrumbs without pressing.
- Fry immediately after breading for maximum crispiness.
- Use oil heated to about 330°F to avoid burning or sogginess.

## Serving Suggestions
Serve schnitzel with:
- French fries (Pommes)
- Spätzle and gravy
- German potato salad
- Creamy German cucumber salad
- Krautsalat (cabbage salad)
- Leafy green salad

Additional sides:
- Mashed or roasted potatoes
- Sauerkraut
- Rotkohl (sweet and sour red cabbage)
- Roasted or steamed vegetables
- Creamy coleslaw, macaroni salad, classic potato salad, baked potato wedges, baked beans, or broccoli salad

Enjoy your perfectly crispy and delicious German Schnitzel!

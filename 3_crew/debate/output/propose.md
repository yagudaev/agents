Ladies and gentlemen, the motion before us today isn't about losing an identity, but about forging a destiny. The proposition that Canada should join the United States is not a matter of conquest, but of common sense and the ultimate realization of a shared future.

Let us be clear on the immense and immediate benefits.

First, we would create an economic superpower the world has never seen. Imagine a single, unified economy stretching from the Arctic Circle to the Rio Grande. We would eliminate all trade friction, unleashing trillions in economic potential. The combined energy resources—from Texas oil to Albertan oil sands to Quebec's hydropower—would create absolute energy independence, freeing us from the volatility of global markets and the influence of hostile regimes. A single currency and unified market would lower prices for consumers and create unparalleled opportunities for businesses on both sides of the current border.

Second, this union guarantees our collective security in an increasingly dangerous world. Together, we would form an unassailable continental fortress. We would present a single, unified front in the strategic Arctic, ending petty disputes and asserting undeniable sovereignty against Russian and Chinese ambitions. NORAD is already a testament to the fact that our defense is indivisible; political union is the next logical step to ensure the permanent safety and security of our people.

Finally, this is about unlocking human potential. For too long, an arbitrary line on a map has limited the free movement of talent, ideas, and ambition. This union would allow any citizen to live, work, and study anywhere in this vast new nation, creating a dynamic talent pool and a meritocracy of opportunity. We share fundamental values of democracy, liberty, and the rule of law. By combining our strengths, we don't diminish our heritage; we build a more powerful, prosperous, and secure union for generations to come. This is not about one country taking over another; it is about two brothers, already allies and friends, deciding to finally build one home together. It is the bold, logical, and necessary step for our time.
